    Definitions and provenance

    Metric definitions
    Accuracy
    Share of all organizations classified correctly. Misleading on its own here — always predicting "not cited" already scores well because positives are rare.
    Precision
    Of the organizations the model flags, the share that really are cited.
    Recall
    Of the organizations that really are cited, the share the model recovers.
    F1
    Harmonic mean of precision and recall — the selection metric on this page.
    ROC-AUC
    Probability that a randomly chosen cited organization is ranked above a randomly chosen uncited one. 0.5 is random ranking; it is threshold-independent.
    PSI
    Population Stability Index between two distributions of the same variable. Under 0.10 stable, 0.10–0.25 moderate, above 0.25 material.
    Leave-one-out CV
    Each organization is held out once and predicted by a model fitted on the other rows, so every reported score is out-of-fold.
